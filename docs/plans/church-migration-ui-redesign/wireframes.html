Togather · Product Direction · Wireframes v1

Make Togather feel like home to a WhatsApp church

Sixteen annotated screens — twelve mobile, four web. The shell mirrors the WhatsApp Communities mental model churches already live in; Togather's superpowers surface inside familiar rooms instead of foreign tabs; and migration becomes a product with an invite kit, member pre-import, and a temporary WhatsApp bridge. Companions in this folder: README.md (brief) and FEATURE-MAP.md (exhaustive feature-inventory → new-home audit).

Hierarchy reconciliationThe two products nest in opposite directions — stage the depth, don't flatten it

Chats — everything in your life, one flat list (62 unread)→ Chats tab — everything in your church. Same shape, tighter scope; scope is the feature.
Community — one item inside the list→ Your church = the whole app (branding, subdomain) + the Community tab.
Community group — a group is one chat thread→ Group — one row until it grows channels. Single-channel groups are indistinguishable from WhatsApp groups.
— no equivalent —→ Channels (general · leaders · announcements · custom · shared · event). Leader-side opt-in; members inherit sub-rows only when a second channel is visible to them.
Many communities interleaved in one list→ Community switcher (Chats-header avatar + You tab) — one church at a time, zero foreign unreads.

W1Chats — the new home

  • WhatsApp Chats anatomy: large title, search, Archived row, stacked-card community avatar, unread badges, green timestamps.
  • Rule 1, one row = one place you talk: Young Adults (single channel) is a plain row, exactly like a WhatsApp group; Worship Team has grown a leaders channel, so it clusters like a WhatsApp Communities row. Joan is a DM, interleaved as WhatsApp users expect.
  • Rule 2: channels appear only when a second one is visible to you — a migrated church starts all-plain-rows. Rule 3: the LG header avatar is the community switcher; one church at a time, zero foreign unreads.
  • Message requests (Signal-style) and thread-bump ordering carry over from the existing inbox.

W11Migration wizard — invite kit step

  • Five steps wrapping the existing demo-community onboarding: church setup → mirror WhatsApp groups → CSV/contacts pre-import → invite kit → go live. Each mirrored group starts with exactly one visible channel (Rule 2), so day one looks like WhatsApp.

  • Phone-number sign-in is the migration cheat code: members keep their identity, zero account setup.
